## The key grant point: reach >> followers
Content is distributed far beyond existing audiences (view-to-follower ratios):
- **germany.wtf** — 947K views from 862 followers (~1,099×)
- **italy_wtf** — 3.1M views from 3,368 followers (~920×)
- **spainwtf** — 1.1M views from 1,469 followers (~749×)
- **england.wtf** — 43K views from 82 followers (~524×)
- **netherlands.wtf** — 1M views from ~2,400 followers (~417×)

This demonstrates the network reaches new audiences on merit, not just its follower base — the strongest distribution argument for the grant.

## Data-quality / honesty notes (for the application)
- **Instagram only** — cross-platform total is higher; label figures as "Instagram alone."
- **Rounded inputs** — Instagram displays views/reach as rounded K/M; totals are therefore approximate (±).
- **Reach not deduplicated** — combined viewer/reach total counts cross-page overlap; state as "combined reach across accounts."
- **Two metric labels** — "Viewers" vs "Accounts reached" are near-equivalent Instagram metrics; noted per row.
- **Duplicates in export set** handled: `thewestwtf` appeared twice (identical) → counted once; `europewtf`/`usamericawtf` "(1)" copies → counted once.
