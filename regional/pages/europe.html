Europe's Worldview

"The great experiment — democracy, unity, and the cracks appearing."

The European Union was built on the ruins of two world wars as a guarantee it would never happen again. Today it faces rising nationalism, democratic backsliding, a migration crisis, and economic fractures between north and south. WatchTheFall Europe documents the strain on the continent's greatest achievement.
