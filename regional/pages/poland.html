Poland's Worldview

"Rising from ruin — and arguing about which direction to fall."

Poland has one of Europe's greatest stories of economic transformation since 1989. It also had eight years under a government that systematically dismantled judicial independence, suppressed press freedom, and weaponised social division. WatchTheFall Poland documents the fight for what kind of country it wants to become.
