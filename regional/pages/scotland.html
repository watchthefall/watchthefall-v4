Scotland's Worldview

"A small nation with the wealth to thrive — choosing to decline."

Scotland sits on oil reserves, renewable energy potential, and a highly educated population. Yet it exports talent, imports dependency, and argues about constitutional questions while its public services collapse. WatchTheFall Scotland documents the gap between what Scotland could be and what it's becoming.
