Canada's Worldview

"The polite decline — too nice to admit it's happening."

Behind the branding it's experiencing the same housing crisis, the same affordability squeeze, and its own unresolved history with Indigenous peoples. WatchTheFall Canada documents the gap between the identity and the reality.
